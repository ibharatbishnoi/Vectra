Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what information Vectra Enterprises, First Floor, Shop No. 135 Wing, Opp. Kataria Show Room, Makarpura, Vadodara, Gujarat – 390014 (“we”, “us”) collects through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Quotation and call-back requests: your name or company, mobile number, and (if you choose to give them) email address, GSTIN, delivery location, notes, and the products, quantities, sizes and makes you ask for.
  • Attachments: photos, drawings or PDFs you choose to upload with a request.
  • Browser storage: your quotation list, recently viewed products and recent enquiry references are saved in your browser so they are still there when you return. Contact details you type are kept only for the current browser tab session.

How we use it

We use this information to prepare and send quotations, contact you about your requirement, let you track and respond to a quotation, and keep a record of our dealings with you. We do not sell your information.

Where it is stored

Requests and attachments are stored in our database and file storage, hosted by Supabase. The website is hosted on Vercel. Only authorised Vectra staff can view requests.

Analytics

If analytics is enabled on this site, we use Google Analytics to count visits and button clicks (for example, calls and WhatsApp taps). It does not receive the contents of your enquiry.

WhatsApp and phone

If you tap a WhatsApp or call button, the message or call is handled by those services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry and quotation records for as long as needed for business, accounting and tax purposes. To ask us to correct or delete your details, contact us at vectra2029@yahoo.com or +91 75975 77129. You can clear your saved list and history at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
